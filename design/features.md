# UNSAID – Core Features & Signature Differentiators

## Core Feature Set

### 7.1 Private Conversation
- Encrypted one-to-one AI companion chat — no public feed
- Text + optional voice input; voice transcribed locally, original audio deleted immediately by default
- **"Do not remember this chat"** switch at conversation level
- One-tap clear / crypto-delete for a selected conversation
- Export selected content only in an encrypted package the user controls

### 7.2 Emotional Check-in
- 10–20 second check-in: mood, body sensations, energy, social connection, safety
- Optional note: *"What made today heavier/lighter?"*
- Weekly pattern view with plain-language observations — **no diagnostic labels**
- User can delete history or turn tracking off completely

### 7.3 The "I Am Blank" Toolkit

| Tool | Example output |
|---|---|
| Say it for me | "I'm not sure how to explain this, but I have been overwhelmed and I don't need advice yet." |
| Give me options | Short / honest / boundary-setting / formal / affectionate |
| Role-play | AI plays friend, parent, professor, interviewer, partner — with a visible "simulation" label |
| One question only | AI asks the smallest possible next question instead of producing an essay |
| Body first | 30–60 second grounding flow before returning to the conversation |

### 7.4 "What Can I Do Right Now?"
Returns **one small action first**, not a 20-step self-improvement list.

Examples: drink water, move to a quieter place, name five things you see, write the one sentence you are avoiding, message a trusted person, book professional support.

> Action library is tagged by intent and risk level.

## Signature Differentiators

### 01 · Memory Dial
Every sentence that could become a long-term memory is user-controlled:
- **Never remember**
- **Remember for 7 days**
- **Remember until I delete it**
- **Save to my vault**

Directly addresses the fear that an AI quietly builds a permanent psychological profile.

---

### 02 · Privacy Receipt
After a sensitive session, the app shows a tiny ledger:
- What was stored
- What was deleted
- Whether cloud inference was used
- Which device holds the key
- What can be shared

Privacy becomes **visible**, not buried in policy text.

---

### 03 · Blank Mode
A dedicated mode for the exact moment the brain feels empty.

Reduces the interface to:
- One question
- Sentence fragments
- Voice capture
- Selectable reply tiles

Goal: **expression**, not perfect wording.

---

### 04 · Conversation Gym (Rehearsal)
A rehearsal environment for difficult conversations.

User chooses:
- The person (friend, parent, professor, partner…)
- Goal of the conversation
- Emotional intensity
- What they fear the other person will say

After rehearsal, the app separates:
- **What I can control**
- **What I cannot**

---

### 05 · Body Alarm Translator
When users report racing heart, nausea, shaking, chest discomfort, or dizziness:
1. Performs a brief **safety/medical screen** first
2. Avoids assuming the cause
3. When appropriate: calm, non-diagnostic explanation + grounding

> NICE guidance: physical problems should be excluded when people present with panic-like symptoms.

---

### 06 · Rumination Brake
Detects and interrupts looping thought patterns — redirects without dismissing.

---

## Notification Design

- Notifications must never feel intrusive or guilt-inducing
- User-controlled frequency and type
- No "streak broken" or loss-framing notifications
- Gentle follow-up after Blank Mode / crisis interactions

---

## Anti-Dependency Guardrails

The app actively works against over-reliance:
- Surfaces "have you talked to someone you trust?" prompts
- Celebrates real-world actions over app actions
- Escalation paths always visible, not hidden behind premium tiers
